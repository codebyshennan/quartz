## Goal
Run a realistic Apr-Jun cut that reduces visceral fat, improves Hyrox running, and preserves muscle.

## Phase targets
- Start (2026-04-24): 77.05kg, 26.1% body fat, visceral fat 12
- Jun 30 target: <=72kg, ~20-22% body fat, visceral fat <=8
- Stretch: <72kg with no obvious strength drop

## Non-negotiables
- [ ] Average calorie intake ~1900 kcal/day
- [ ] Protein 150-160g/day
- [ ] Creatine 5g daily
- [ ] 2 dedicated runs/week
- [ ] 2 strength-preserving sessions/week
- [ ] Weigh in 4x/week on waking; track weekly average
- [ ] Re-scan in mid-Jun

## Daily nutrition targets
- Calories: 1900 kcal
- Protein: 150-160g
- Carbs: 170-210g
- Fat: 45-60g
- Fibre: 25g+
- Water: 2.5-3.5L

## Simple meal structure
### Breakfast options
1. Eggs + oats
   - 3 eggs
   - 80g oats with water
   - 1 fruit
   - Approx: 500 kcal / 28-32g protein
2. Fast breakfast shake
   - 1 scoop whey
   - 60g oats
   - 1 banana
   - 5g creatine
   - Approx: 430 kcal / 32-35g protein
3. Greek yogurt bowl
   - 250g Greek yogurt
   - 40g oats or granola
   - berries / banana
   - Approx: 350-450 kcal / 25-30g protein

- 1 palm and a half to 2 palms lean protein

  - shrimp
  - lean beef
- 1 fist carbs on rest days, 1.5-2 fists on run / hard training days
  - rice
  - potatoes
  - oats
  - rice noodles
- 2 fists vegetables
- 1 thumb fats if meal is very lean
  - olive oil
  - avocado
  - nuts

## Eating out rules
- Pho / bun bo / hu tieu: add extra meat, finish protein first, do not drink all broth
- Com tam / com ga: request extra meat, less rice, skip sugary drink
- Banh mi: only as backup meal, not a daily default
- Ca phe sua da: max 1/day; better black coffee during cut phase
- Alcohol: cap at 1-2 drinks/week; avoid beer sessions
- Dessert / sweet drinks: keep to 2 planned treats/week max

## Supplements
- Whey: 1-2 scoops/day only to close protein gap
- Creatine monohydrate: 5g daily
- Electrolytes: useful on long-run / high-sweat days

## Progression rules
- Add weight only if all prescribed reps were clean last week
- On runs, do not increase both pace and volume in the same week
- If fatigue is high, reduce Friday conditioning volume by 25-30%
- If bodyweight stalls for 2 weeks, reduce intake by ~100-150 kcal/day or tighten restaurant meals
