## Design Context

### Users
Shen Nan Wong — software engineer sharing his personal Obsidian knowledge garden publicly. Audience is mixed: curious readers following his thinking, and employers/recruiters evaluating his work. Viewed casually on desktop and mobile, both daytime and evening.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, paperlike, systematic — the feel of a well-designed technical book or literary journal, not a tech startup landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean paperlike editorial. Light and dark modes both supported. Warm cream paper tones for light, warm ink-dark for dark. Accent color: terracotta/sienna — distinctive, warm, editorial (not the AI cyan/purple palette). No glassmorphism, no gradient text, no left-stripe callouts.

### Typography
- Header: Spectral (editorial serif, screen-optimized, Production Type)
- Body: Literata (long-form reading, TypeTogether)
- Code: JetBrains Mono

### Design Principles
1. Reading comfort first — generous line-height, constrained measure, clear hierarchy
2. Editorial restraint — every decoration must earn its place
3. Warm not cold — palette tinted toward sienna, never pure black/white or cold gray
4. Underline links, not background pills — links should feel like text, not chips
5. Blockquotes as pull-quotes — horizontal rules, italic, never a left stripe
